Judge your answers here

Everything stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded. You do not type commands on this page.

  1. Load the file web_session.json (the terminal printed its path when you ran python3 study.py web).
  2. Compare: for each task, pick the answer you would rather receive and the one you would least like. The labels X, Y, Z do not say how an answer was written.
  3. Keep or delete each skill or principle the tool wrote from your messages.
  4. Choose which of the two sets is the better one to keep and build on.
  5. Save: the page gives you one file, web_results.json. Back in the terminal, run python3 study.py web-import and then export and bundle.
About 12 to 20 minutes. You can close the page and come back; your choices are kept in this browser.

Saved: one last step in the terminal

Your browser saved web_results.json (usually in your Downloads folder). In the terminal, in the kit folder, run these one at a time:

python3 study.py web-import
python3 study.py export
python3 study.py bundle

web-import finds the file in Downloads or in my-study and writes your share files. Then open each file the tool lists, delete any row you do not want to share, and upload the zip as the README says. The results file stays on your computer.
